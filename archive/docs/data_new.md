# Data sources, by question

---

## 1. Where was the coast?

1. British Admiralty hydrographic surveys.
2. Dutch and French coastal charts.
3. Historical maps from the David Rumsey collection.
4. Landsat imagery, before the dams and the barrage.
5. Global Surface Water, 1984 to 2021.
6. Sentinel-2 imagery.
7. Ptolemy's coordinates, the only surviving ancient cartography of these coasts.

**To do.** Further French and Dutch charts covering the candidate sites. The
Tabula Peutingeriana. A published sea level curve for Kerala. Survey of India
sheets.

---

## 2. How deep was the water?

1. Depth soundings on the Dutch charts.
2. Tide gauge records.
3. Global seabed grids, which hold no measurement on these coasts.

**To do.** Depth soundings on the British Admiralty sheets. ICESat-2 lidar
bathymetry. Single beam survey tracks. Published sediment cores carrying depth
sensitive microfossils.

---

## 3. Where did the rivers run?

1. Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery.
2. L band radar.
3. The Copernicus elevation model.
4. Global Surface Water, as a record of rivers that dried between 1984 and 2021.
5. Published boreholes and radiocarbon dates from Kerala.
6. Published radiocarbon dates from the Rufiji delta and Zanzibar.

**To do.** An electrical resistivity survey of the Periyar basin. The Kuttanad
borehole set.

---

## 4. How fast did ships sail?

1. NCEP reanalysis wind records, 1948 to present.
2. Published performance curves for square-rigged vessels.
3. Ship tonnage from the Muziris papyrus.
4. Sailing distances and durations stated in the Periplus Maris Erythraei.

**To do.** Palaeoclimate records of monsoon strength in the Roman period.

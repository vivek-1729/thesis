# What I need from you

Everything here is blocked on a login, a library proxy, a browser, or a decision.
Nothing on this list can be done from the terminal. Ordered by how much it
unblocks.

---

## 1. Accounts, about twenty minutes total

**USGS EarthExplorer**, free. https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/
Unlocks CORONA, the declassified 1960s imagery. Once registered I can search,
order and process frames. This is the sharpest pre-dam epoch available, two to
eight metres against Landsat's seventy-nine.

**AVISO for FES2022**, or **TPXO**, free for research.
Tidal range at each port. Without it the navigability work is guesswork, because
whether a ship clears a bar depends on the state of the tide, not just the mean
depth.

**Google Earth Engine**, free for academic use, approval takes a day or two.
Not needed for anything yet. Worth starting now only because if we ever sweep a
whole basin rather than a 20 km box, we will want it and will not want to wait.

## 2. Papers, one ScienceDirect session

All through the Harvard library proxy. Save each with the filename in bold into
`library/geomorphology/`. Full citations and DOIs are in `TO-DOWNLOAD.md`.

**`priju-2018-periyar-paleochannels.pdf`** — the single most valuable item on
this list. Maps buried channels in the Periyar basin by electrical resistivity,
which sees through the engineered surface that defeated all four of our methods.

**`narayana-2001-periyar-paleodelta.pdf`** — the palaeodelta at the Periyar
mouth, about 50 km², up to 6 km wide. The Muziris landscape.

**`mathai-nair-1988-cochin-kodungallur-coast.pdf`** — the source of the claim
that the Kerala coast was "almost completed by 2000 years BP". Load-bearing, and
the later literature cites it without rechecking. ias.ac.in serves it free in a
browser but blocks scripts.

**`maya-2022-kuttanad-boreholes.pdf`** — the five Kuttanad boreholes by name,
in the Nelkynda and Bakarē zone.

**`alappat-2015-osl-beach-ridges.pdf`** — OSL rather than radiocarbon, so it may
reach the first century where our radiocarbon dates miss by five hundred years.

**`shaji-2022-sea-level.pdf`**, **`padmalal-2011-kollam-wetlands.pdf`**,
**`padmalal-2013-bayhead-delta.pdf`**, **`tiwari-2025-vembanad-paleolimnology.pdf`**
(open access, MDPI blocks scripts, one click).

**Datoo (1970)**, *Azania* 5: 65–75, via Taylor and Francis. Half an hour, and
it removes a second-hand citation from the Rhapta chapter.

**Turner (1989)**, *Roman Coins from India*. Appendix 1 catalogues single and
stray finds, the only numismatic category bearing on harbour location.

## 3. Reading-room, when you go

**Survey of India one-inch sheets 58 B, C and G**, especially 58 C/NW, surveyed
1911 to 1913. Not online anywhere I could find. They fill the gap between the
1857 charts and the 1960s imagery, and they show the backwaters before the
Kuttanad reclamation.

**ADM 352 at the National Archives**, the manuscript originals behind the
published Admiralty charts, which carry sounding detail the engraved versions
dropped.

**Qatar Digital Library**, qdl.qa, which holds digitised India Office Records
and Admiralty material and blocks programmatic access entirely.

## 5. Decisions only you can make

**Does the voyage simulator estimate its own parameters?** If yes, the ABC
references come back into the statistics reading and the work grows
substantially. If no, the simulator produces a covariate and the three papers
stay as background. Recorded as an open question in `STATISTICAL-PLAN.md`.

**Is Naura in or out?** It sits in the same Periplus sentence as Tyndis, it is
unlocated, its proposals are 135 km apart, and it is on a coast where we have
already built the record. Its absence needs either a stated reason or a
correction.

**TanDEM-X at 12 m**, a short DLR science proposal. Worth requesting only if you
want to know whether terrain can contribute anywhere, since the current failure
is resolution rather than method.

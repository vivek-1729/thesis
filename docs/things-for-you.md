# Things for you

Everything on this list needs a person. Scripts cannot do any of it, either
because it needs a login, a browser, a library card, a decision or a hand.
Ordered by what it costs you, not by what it is worth.

## 2. One email

Write to **priju@cwrdm.org**, Dr C P Priju at the Centre for Water Resources
Development and Management, Kozhikode.

He is the one person holding what `FINDINGS.md` ranks as the highest-value
outstanding item. His 2018 chapter is the published fragment, but his
institutional page lists two funded projects behind it, one of them DST-SERB
funded and the second completed on 31 December 2022, titled *An integrated study
of Paleo-channel aquifers of Periyar river basin using hydrogeological,
geo-electrical and isotope methods*. Those carry final technical reports with
the sounding locations and sections in them, and none of it is published.

Ask for the palaeochannel traces as coordinates or as a shapefile. State that it
is for a senior thesis on the first-century ports and that he will be cited.
This is the single highest-return action on the list.

## 4. The Harvard proxy, in this order

1. **Priju et al. 2018**, `10.1007/978-981-10-5801-1_27`. Buried channels in the
   Periyar basin by resistivity. Resistivity reads the sand body under the
   surface, which is the one method immune to the thing that defeated four
   surface methods here.
2. **Maya et al. 2022**, `10.1016/b978-0-323-90085-0.00026-7`. The five lower
   Kuttanad boreholes, inside the Nelkynda and Bakarē zone, the only part of the
   coast with no dated subsurface evidence at a candidate.
3. **Shaji et al. 2022**, `10.1016/j.quaint.2022.03.005`. The Kerala sea-level
   curve. There is no substitute, and without it the historic soundings have
   nothing to reduce to.
4. **Mathai and Nair 1988.** The claim that the coast was "almost completed by
   2000 years BP" is load-bearing and has been cited without rechecking ever
   since.
5. **Alappat et al. 2015.**

## 7. Decisions only you can make

- **Is Naura in or out?** It changes the candidate table and every count drawn
  from it.
- **Does the voyage simulator estimate its own parameters**, or take them fixed?
- **The backwater route.** The chart will not give it up automatically, and
  section 6 of `new_findings.md` says why. A hand trace of the main navigable
  route off the georeferenced 1857 sheets is a few hours and would be more
  reliable than anything automated. Your call whether to spend them.
- **Which of the seven ports the thesis argues hardest for.** Six are disputed
  and the evidence is not evenly distributed among them.

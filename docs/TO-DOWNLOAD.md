# Papers to download

Drop every PDF into `library/geomorphology/` using the **filename given in bold**,
and I will extract the cores, dates and coordinates the same way I did for
Maya 2017.

The Elsevier items are one ScienceDirect session through the Harvard library
proxy. The ones marked open access need no proxy at all, only a browser: those
publishers answer a scripted request with a challenge page rather than the file,
which is a block on automation and not on access.

`LITERATURE-SWEEP.md` lists nine more of those, found after this page was
written, and says which gap each one bears on.

## Kerala cores and coastline

**`maya-2022-kuttanad-boreholes.pdf`**
Maya, K., D. Padmalal, M. Vandana, S. Vishnu Mohan, V. R. Vivek, Ruta B. Limaye, and K. P. N. Kumaran. "Holocene Changes in Fluvial Geomorphology, Depositional Environments, and Evolution of Coastal Wetlands: A Multiproxy Study from Southwest India." In *Holocene Climate Change and Environment*, 483–513. Elsevier, 2022.
https://doi.org/10.1016/b978-0-323-90085-0.00026-7
*The five lower Kuttanad boreholes, Nedumudi, Alappuzha, Kaniyamkulam, Thakazhi and Kidangara. The highest-priority item on this list: these sit in the Nelkynda and Bakarē zone.*

**`narayana-2017-paleodelta.pdf`**
Narayana, A. C., Vinu Prakash, P. K. Gautam, and Swati Tripathi. "Holocene Environmental Changes as Recorded in Sediments of a Paleodelta, Southwest Coast of India." *Quaternary International* 443 (2017): 115–23.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quaint.2017.04.016
*A buried delta on this coast is the Muziris question in geological form.*

**`alappat-2015-osl-beach-ridges.pdf`**
Alappat, Linto, Manfred Frechen, S. Sree Kumar, D. S. Suresh Babu, Rajan Ravur, and Sumiko Tsukamoto. "Evidence of Late Holocene Shoreline Progradation in the Coast of Kerala, South India, Obtained from OSL Dating of Palaeo-Beach Ridges." *Geomorphology* 245 (2015): 73–86.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geomorph.2015.05.006
*Successive shoreline positions with dates attached. OSL rather than radiocarbon, so it may cover the horizon the radiocarbon record misses.*

**`shaji-2022-sea-level.pdf`**
Shaji, Jithu, Upasana S. Banerji, K. Maya, Kumar Batuk Joshi, Ankur J. Dabhi, Nisha Bharti, Ravi Bhushan, and D. Padmalal. "Holocene Monsoon and Sea-Level Variability from Coastal Lowlands of Kerala, SW India." *Quaternary International* 642 (2022): 48–62.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quaint.2022.03.005
*The regional relative sea-level curve, which is the datum everything else is measured against.*

**`padmalal-2011-kollam-wetlands.pdf`**
Padmalal, D., K. P. N. Kumaran, K. M. Nair, B. Baijulal, Ruta B. Limaye, and S. Vishnu Mohan. "Evolution of the Coastal Wetland Systems of SW India during the Holocene: Evidence from Marine and Terrestrial Archives of Kollam Coast, Kerala." *Quaternary International* 237, no. 1–2 (2011): 123–39.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quaint.2010.12.021

**`tiwari-2025-vembanad-paleolimnology.pdf`** — open access, just blocked to scripts
Tiwari, Pooja, Biswajeet Thakur, Purnima Srivastava, Sanjay Kumar Singh Gahlaud, Ravi Bhusan, and Rajesh Agnihotri. "Paleolimnology and Natural Versus Anthropogenic Influx During the Late Holocene from Vembanad Wetland, Ramsar Site, Kerala, India." *Quaternary* 8, no. 1 (2025): 3.
https://www.mdpi.com/2571-550X/8/1/3
*Late Holocene specifically, so of the six this is the one most likely to reach the Periplus horizon.*

**`mathai-nair-1988-cochin-kodungallur-coast.pdf`**
Mathai, T., and S. B. Nair. "Stages in the Emergence of the Cochin-Kodungallur Coast: Evidence for the Interaction of Marine and Fluvial Processes." *Proceedings of the Indian Academy of Sciences* 54, no. 3 (1988): 439–47. No DOI; ias.ac.in blocks scripted access but serves free PDFs in a browser.
*The source of the claim that the Kerala coast was "almost completed by 2000 years BP", which if true means the first-century coastline near the Periyar is close to the modern one. A great deal rests on this one paper and nobody in the later literature seems to have rechecked it.*

**`priju-2018-periyar-paleochannels.pdf`** — highest priority of the whole list
Priju, C. P., Jiby Francis, P. R. Arun, and N. B. Narasimha Prasad. "Delineation of Paleochannels in Periyar River Basin of Kerala Using Remote Sensing and Electrical Resistivity Methods." *Water Science and Technology Library* (2018): 391–400.
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-10-5801-1_27
*Someone has already mapped the buried channels of the Periyar basin, and they used electrical resistivity, which sees sand bodies through the engineered surface that defeated every method we tried. This is the paper that may hand us the Muziris channel network.*

**`narayana-2001-periyar-paleodelta.pdf`** — open access, just blocked to scripts
Narayana, A. C., C. P. Priju, and A. Chakrabarti. "Identification of a Palaeodelta near the Mouth of Periyar River in Central Kerala." *Journal of the Geological Society of India* 57, no. 6 (2001): 545–47.
https://doi.org/10.17491/jgsi/2001/570609
*A buried delta of about 50 km², up to 6 km wide, at the Periyar mouth. This is the Muziris landscape. A second DOI, 10.17491/jgsi/2001/580409, carries the same title at 58, no. 4: 368–69 with no authors and is probably a discussion. Worth taking both while you are there.*

**`das-2024-muziris-cores.pdf`** — open access, just blocked to scripts
Das, Prasenjit, D. Padmalal, K. P. N. Kumaran, Ruta B. Limaye, S. Vishnu Mohan, Upasana S. Banerji, and Ravi Bhushan. "Tracing the Late Quaternary Coastal Evolution of Central Kerala, India, around the Lost Ancient Port Muziris Using Multi-Proxy Study of the Sedimentary Archives." *Quaternary Science Advances* 14 (2024): 100197.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.qsa.2024.100197
*Three drilled cores at the Periyar mouth, with Holocene sediment down to 1.1 cal kyr BP. That is the youngest dated material anyone has reported on this coast, and about 1,350 years younger than the floor of the cores we hold.*

**`padmalal-2013-bayhead-delta.pdf`**
Padmalal, D., K. M. Nair, K. P. N. Kumaran, K. Sajan, S. Vishnu Mohan, K. Maya, V. Santhosh, S. Anooja, and R. Limaye. "Climate and Sea Level Changes in a Holocene Bay Head Delta, Kerala, Southwest Coast of India." In *Climate Change and Island and Coastal Vulnerability*, 191–208. 2013.
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-94-007-6016-5_13

## Not geomorphology, but the other acquisitions worth making

**Datoo (1970).** "Rhapta: the Location and Importance of East Africa's First Port." *Azania* 5: 65–75. https://doi.org/10.1080/00672707009511528
*Harvard has Taylor and Francis. Half an hour, and it removes a second-hand citation from the Rhapta chapter.*

**Turner (1989).** *Roman Coins from India*. Routledge reissue, ISBN 9780367605827.
*Appendix 1 catalogues single and stray finds, the only numismatic category that bears on harbour location and the one CHRE omits.*

# What to read next

Geographic first, because the Periplus gives the Malabar distances by river and
by sea and nothing can be measured against a candidate until the channels are
reconstructed. `READING-GEOGRAPHIC.md` holds the fuller bibliography.

---

## Geographic: read these three

**Maya, Padmalal, Vandana, Vishnu Mohan, Vivek, Limaye and Kumaran (2022).** "Holocene Changes in Fluvial Geomorphology, Depositional Environments, and Evolution of Coastal Wetlands: A Multiproxy Study from Southwest India." In *Holocene Climate Change and Environment*, 483–513. [doi](https://doi.org/10.1016/b978-0-323-90085-0.00026-7)

The five lower Kuttanad boreholes, Nedumudi, Alappuzha, Kaniyamkulam, Thakazhi and Kidangara. The only dated evidence at the candidates themselves, and it sits in the Nelkynda and Bakarē zone.

**Narayana, Prakash, Gautam and Tripathi (2017).** "Holocene Environmental Changes as Recorded in Sediments of a Paleodelta, Southwest Coast of India." *Quaternary International* 443: 115–23. [doi](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quaint.2017.04.016)

A palaeodelta on this coast is the Muziris question in geological form.

**Giosan, Constantinescu, Clift, Tabrez, Danish and Inam (2006).** "Recent Morphodynamics of the Indus Delta Shore and Shelf." *Continental Shelf Research* 26 (14): 1668–84. [doi](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csr.2006.05.009)

Nineteenth-century Admiralty surveys digitised into a terrain model. Our step 2, already executed by someone else.

## Classics: read these two

**Datoo (1970).** "Rhapta: the Location and Importance of East Africa's First Port." *Azania* 5: 65–75. [doi](https://doi.org/10.1080/00672707009511528)

Every Rhapta argument inherits his Pangani to Rufiji bracket and we still cite it at second hand. Harvard has Taylor and Francis, so this is a half-hour job.

**[Gawlikowski, Juchniewicz and al-Zahrani (2021), Aynuna.](../../library/excavation-reports/Gawlikowski-Juchniewicz-al-Zahrani-2021-Aynuna-Nabataean-Port-Red-Sea.pdf)**

Held and only skimmed. 450 pp, and the coin catalogue is the decisive evidence for Leukē Kōmē.

### Excavation reports held but unread

- [Sedov (1997), Qana](../../library/excavation-reports/apa-IndOc-Gulf-Qana-Sedov1997.pdf) and [Avanzini (2011), Sumhuram](../../library/excavation-reports/apa-IndOc-Gulf-Sumhuram-Avanzini2011.pdf) — the two best-quantified secure ports, so they define what a confirmed Periplus port looks like. Avanzini also covers the Khor Rori lagoon silting.
- [Smith and Wright (1988), Ras Hafun](../../library/excavation-reports/apa-IndOc-Gulf-RasHafun-Smith1988.pdf) — the only secure port on the western route south of Adulis, so the whole calibration set for Rhapta.
- [Peacock and Blue (2011), Myos Hormos](../../library/excavation-reports/apa-RedSea-MyosHormos-PeacockandBlue2011.pdf) — 387 pp, the other anchor.

### Still to acquire

- **Turner (1989), *Roman Coins from India*.** Routledge reissue ISBN 9780367605827. Appendix 1 catalogues single and stray finds, the category CHRE omits and the only one bearing on harbour location.
- **F. A. Khan, *Banbhore: A Preliminary Report*.** The original excavation, and the reason we cannot say whether virgin soil was reached. Sweep the rest of *Pakistan Archaeology* from doam.gov.pk at the same time.
- **Salles and Sedov, eds. (2010), *Qāni'. Le port antique du Hadramawt*.** Brepols, 553 pp, print only. Several hundred bronzes, a second quantified anchor beside Pattanam.

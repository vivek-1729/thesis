# What to read next

The queue, in the order it unblocks work. Geographic first, because the Periplus
gives the Malabar distances by river and by sea and nothing can be measured
against a candidate until the channels are reconstructed.

`READING-GEOGRAPHIC.md` holds the fuller bibliography. This file is only what to
read now and in what order.

---

## Part 1. Geographic reconstruction

### Priority one: the Kerala cores

The only dated evidence at the candidates themselves, covering four of the seven
ports, and we hold none of it. Acquiring and extracting these is the cheapest
real progress available.

**Holocene Changes in Fluvial Geomorphology, Depositional Environments, and Evolution of Coastal Wetlands: A Multiproxy Study from Southwest India.** Maya, Padmalal, Vandana, Vishnu Mohan, Vivek, Limaye and Kumaran (2022). In *Holocene Climate Change and Environment*, 483–513. [doi:10.1016/b978-0-323-90085-0.00026-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/b978-0-323-90085-0.00026-7)

The five lower Kuttanad boreholes: Nedumudi, Alappuzha, Kaniyamkulam, Thakazhi and Kidangara. That is the Nelkynda and Bakarē zone directly. **Read this first.**

**Holocene Environmental Changes as Recorded in Sediments of a Paleodelta, Southwest Coast of India.** Narayana, Prakash, Gautam and Tripathi (2017). *Quaternary International* 443: 115–23. [doi:10.1016/j.quaint.2017.04.016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quaint.2017.04.016)

A palaeodelta on this coast is the Muziris question in geological form.

**Geomorphic Response to Sea Level and Climate Changes during Late Quaternary in a Humid Tropical Coastline.** Maya, Vishnu Mohan, Limaye, Padmalal and Kumaran (2017). *PLOS ONE* 12 (5): e0176775. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0176775](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0176775)

Open access, and the terrain evolution model the borehole work sits inside.

**Evolution of the Coastal Wetland Systems of SW India during the Holocene: Kollam Coast.** Padmalal, Kumaran, Nair, Baijulal, Limaye and Vishnu Mohan (2011). *Quaternary International* 237 (1–2): 123–39. [doi:10.1016/j.quaint.2010.12.021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quaint.2010.12.021)

**Evidence of Late Holocene Shoreline Progradation in the Coast of Kerala, from OSL Dating of Palaeo-Beach Ridges.** Alappat, Frechen, Sree Kumar, Suresh Babu, Ravur and Tsukamoto (2015). *Geomorphology* 245: 73–86. [doi:10.1016/j.geomorph.2015.05.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geomorph.2015.05.006)

Successive shoreline positions with dates attached.

**Holocene Monsoon and Sea-Level Variability from Coastal Lowlands of Kerala.** Shaji, Banerji, Maya, Joshi, Dabhi, Bharti, Bhushan and Padmalal (2022). *Quaternary International* 642: 48–62. [doi:10.1016/j.quaint.2022.03.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quaint.2022.03.005)

The regional relative sea-level curve, which is the datum everything else is measured against.

### Priority two: the methods

**Large-Scale, Multi-Temporal Remote Sensing of Palaeo-River Networks.** Orengo and Petrie (2017). *Remote Sensing* 9 (7): 735. [doi:10.3390/rs9070735](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs9070735)

How dead channels are recovered, and the output stays a probability per pixel rather than a line on a map.

**Recent Morphodynamics of the Indus Delta Shore and Shelf.** Giosan, Constantinescu, Clift, Tabrez, Danish and Inam (2006). *Continental Shelf Research* 26 (14): 1668–84. [doi:10.1016/j.csr.2006.05.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csr.2006.05.009)

Nineteenth-century Admiralty surveys digitised into a terrain model to measure delta change before the barrages. This is our step 2, already executed by someone else.

**Fluvial Landscapes of the Harappan Civilization.** Giosan, Clift, Macklin, Fuller, Constantinescu, Durcan, Stevens et al. (2012). *PNAS* 109 (26). [doi:10.1073/pnas.1112743109](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1112743109)

### Priority three: the other three ports

**Holocene Shelf Sedimentation Patterns off Equatorial East Africa.** Liu, Rendle-Bühring, Meyer and Henrich (2016). *Sedimentary Geology* 331: 1–11. [doi:10.1016/j.sedgeo.2015.10.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sedgeo.2015.10.009) — Rhapta.

**Northern Hemisphere Control of Deglacial Vegetation Changes in the Rufiji Uplands.** Bouimetarhan, Dupont, Kuhlmann, Pätzold, Prange, Schefuß and Zonneveld (2015). *Climate of the Past* 11: 751–64. [doi:10.5194/cp-11-751-2015](https://doi.org/10.5194/cp-11-751-2015) — the GeoB12624 core off the Rufiji mouth.

**Late Holocene Red Sea Coastal Evolution, North Al-Wajh.** Ghandour, Majeed, Al-Zubieri, Mannaa, Aljahdali and Bantan (2020). *Thalassas* 37 (1): 1–12. [doi:10.1007/s41208-020-00248-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41208-020-00248-2) — the wadi-mouth lagoon analogue for Aynuna.

**Holocene Sea Levels at the Gulf of Aqaba.** Weil, Stein, Shaked, Lazar and Agnon (2022). *Quaternary Science Reviews* 277: 107278. [doi:10.1016/j.quascirev.2021.107278](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quascirev.2021.107278) — the Red Sea datum. Read alongside Manaa et al. (2016), [doi:10.1016/j.margeo.2016.02.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.margeo.2016.02.002), on the tectonic uplift that has to be subtracted first.

## Part 2. Classics

### Held, unread, in order of what they settle

[Gawlikowski, Juchniewicz and al-Zahrani 2021, Aynuna](../../library/excavation-reports/Gawlikowski-Juchniewicz-al-Zahrani-2021-Aynuna-Nabataean-Port-Red-Sea.pdf) — 450 pp. The coin catalogue is the decisive evidence for Leukē Kōmē and we have only skimmed it.

[Sedov 1997, Qana](../../library/excavation-reports/apa-IndOc-Gulf-Qana-Sedov1997.pdf) and [Avanzini 2011, Sumhuram](../../library/excavation-reports/apa-IndOc-Gulf-Sumhuram-Avanzini2011.pdf) — the two best-quantified secure ports, so they define what a confirmed Periplus port looks like materially. Avanzini also covers the Khor Rori lagoon silting, the one place a harbour environment has actually been reconstructed.

[Smith and Wright 1988, Ras Hafun](../../library/excavation-reports/apa-IndOc-Gulf-RasHafun-Smith1988.pdf) — the only secure port on the western route south of Adulis, so it is the whole calibration set for Rhapta.

[Peacock and Blue 2011, Myos Hormos](../../library/excavation-reports/apa-RedSea-MyosHormos-PeacockandBlue2011.pdf) — 387 pp, the other anchor.

### To acquire

**Rhapta: the Location and Importance of East Africa's First Port.** Datoo (1970). *Azania* 5: 65–75. [doi:10.1080/00672707009511528](https://doi.org/10.1080/00672707009511528)

Every Rhapta argument inherits his Pangani to Rufiji bracket and we still cite it at second hand. Harvard has Taylor and Francis, so this is a half-hour job.

**Roman Coins from India.** Turner (1989). Routledge reissue ISBN 9780367605827.

Appendix 1 catalogues single and stray finds, the category CHRE omits and the only one that bears on harbour location.

**Banbhore: A Preliminary Report.** F. A. Khan. Department of Archaeology and Museums, Pakistan.

The original excavation, and the reason we cannot say whether virgin soil was ever reached. Sweep the other volumes of *Pakistan Archaeology* from doam.gov.pk at the same time.

**Qāni'. Le port antique du Hadramawt.** Salles and Sedov, eds. (2010). Brepols, 553 pp. Print only.

Several hundred bronzes, a second quantified anchor beside Pattanam.
